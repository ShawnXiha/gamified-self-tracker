# User Guide — Gamified Self-Tracker

## Getting Started

Open the app in your browser. The first time you visit, you'll see an empty habit list.

### Install on Your Phone (Recommended)

On Android Chrome or iOS Safari, tap "Add to Home Screen" to install as a PWA. The app works offline and opens in full-screen mode like a native app.

## Core Loop

The daily cycle has four steps:

1. **Plan** — Create habits you want to track
2. **Check In** — Tap the checkbox when you complete a habit
3. **Score** — See your XP, level, and streak update instantly
4. **Reflect** — On Monday, review your Weekly Summary

## Habits

### Creating a Habit

Type a habit name (e.g. "Drink Water") in the input field and click **Add**. Your habit appears in the list immediately.

### Daily Check-In

Each habit shows a checkbox. Tap it once to mark it as completed for today. Tap again to undo.

When you check in:
- A **+XP toast** floats up showing the XP you earned
- Your **streak flame** updates with your current streak count
- Your **level progress bar** fills up

### Important Rules

- Each habit can be checked in **once per day**
- The day resets at **04:00 AM** (not midnight). If you check in at 2 AM, it counts for the previous day.
- You **cannot** retroactively check in for past days

## Understanding Your Stats

### Level & XP

You earn XP for every habit you complete:

| Streak Days | XP Earned |
|-------------|-----------|
| 0 (first day) | 10 XP |
| 1–5 | 10 + (streak × 2) XP |
| 5+ | 20 XP (capped) |

XP accumulates toward your next level. The progress bar at the top shows how close you are.

### Streaks

Your streak counts **consecutive days** you've checked in a habit. If you miss a day:

- A **Streak Freeze** is automatically consumed and your streak survives
- If you have no freezes left, your streak resets to 0

### Streak Freezes

Freezes are your safety net for life's interruptions:

- **Start with 2** freezes
- **Earn 1 freeze** each time you level up
- **Earn 1 freeze** for every 7 consecutive days of active check-ins
- **Maximum 5** freezes at any time
- Freezes are consumed automatically when you miss a day

### Streak Flame

A fire emoji appears next to habits with an active streak. The number shows your current streak count.

## Weekly Summary

Every **Monday**, a modal appears showing your past 7 days:

| Stat | What It Means |
|------|---------------|
| Habits Completed | How many check-ins you made vs. total possible |
| Consistency Rate | Percentage of scheduled habits you completed |
| Longest Streak | Your best streak across all habits |
| Total Lifetime Days | How many unique days you've been active |
| Freezes Remaining | Your current streak freeze balance |

Dismiss the summary to close it. It won't appear again that day.

## Tips

- **Start small** — 2–3 habits is plenty for your first week
- **Check in right after doing** — don't wait until the end of the day
- **Don't stress about streaks** — freezes exist for a reason
- **Monday review** — use the weekly summary to spot patterns
