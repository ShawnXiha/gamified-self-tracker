# Daily Feedback Template

**For Beta Testers:** Please use this format to message your Beta Coordinator quickly each day.

## Format
**[Day #] Feedback**

1. **Did you open the app and check in today?**
   - Yes / No
2. **Did you notice your streak and/or XP increase?**
   - Yes / No
3. **What was the biggest friction point today?**
   - [Anything confusing, annoying, broken, or not working as expected? Keep it brief!]

## Example Message
"Day 2 Feedback
1. Yes, checked off 2 habits.
2. Yes, saw my streak flame hit '2' today.
3. The checkbox was a little hard to tap on my tiny screen, but otherwise fine."
