# Beta Tester Guide
**Welcome to the Gamified Self-Tracker Beta!**

Thank you for helping us test the MVP (Minimum Viable Product). Our goal is to see if adding a little bit of XP and a streak flame can help you build better daily habits.

## What is this app?
It is a simple, fast, privacy-first habit tracker. 
**Important:** The app is "local-first." This means **all your data lives on your device** (in your browser). 
* Do not use Incognito/Private mode (your data will be wiped when you close it).
* Do not switch between your phone and your laptop (they will not sync).
* Pick ONE device (preferably your phone) and use that for the entire beta test.

## Your 3-Day Mission
We are testing this app over **3 days**. Please follow this schedule:

### Day 1: Setup & Activation
1. **Open the App:** [Insert Beta URL Here]
2. **Install It (Optional but recommended):** 
   * *iOS (Safari):* Tap the Share icon at the bottom -> "Add to Home Screen".
   * *Android (Chrome):* Tap the 3 dots at the top right -> "Install App" or "Add to Home Screen".
3. **Add 2 Simple Habits:** Keep them incredibly easy to do (e.g., "Drink 1 glass of water", "Stretch for 1 minute").
4. **Check In:** Tap the checkboxes to mark them complete for today. Notice your XP and Streak.

### Day 2: The Return
1. **Open the App:** Tap the icon on your home screen or return to the URL.
2. **Check In:** Complete your habits and check them off. Watch your streak flame ignite!
3. **Daily Feedback:** Send a quick 2-minute message to the Beta Coordinator.

### Day 3: Resilience & Feedback
1. **Open the App:** Try leaving one habit unchecked, but check off the other.
2. **Explore:** Tap on things, see what happens.
3. **Final Step:** Fill out the Final Beta Feedback Form provided by the coordinator.
